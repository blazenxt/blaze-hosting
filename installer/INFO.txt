Blaze Hosting — isolated native client

What this installer puts on your PC
  • BlazeHosting.exe — Windows app (not a website in a window)
  • Start Menu and Desktop shortcuts
  • Uninstaller

What it does
  • Create an account or sign in
  • Manage only YOUR servers on panel.nawazfx.com
  • Console, files, environment variables (.env auto-detect),
    backups, databases, network, settings, API keys

What it never does
  • Open another origin
  • Show another user's files or servers
